Support

Title Shot: MMA Career Sim for iOS and Android

Write to mert@maindtec.de. It is a real person and a small game, so you will get a real answer.

Reporting a bug

The most useful thing you can send is the seed. Every career is reproducible from its seed, its starting picks and the choices made, so with those the exact career you were playing can be replayed here and the bug seen directly.

The seed is printed on the card at the end of a career. A screenshot of that card is perfect. If you have not finished the career, tell us roughly how far you had got (your record, your age and the promotion you were fighting in) and what you had just tapped.

Also helpful: your phone model and OS version, and a screenshot if something looked wrong rather than behaved wrong.

My career disappeared

There is one save at a time. Beginning a new career replaces the one before it, and that cannot be undone from inside the game. Finished careers stay listed in your hall of fame on the start screen.

A career can also be declined on purpose after an update. The save works by replaying your choices through the game’s writing and rules, so if those have changed, the old list of choices would rebuild a different career rather than yours. The game refuses that instead of handing you a stranger’s. It is the right behaviour and it is still annoying; updates that change the event bank or the fight rules will be flagged in the release notes.

“I got robbed”

Maybe, but not by the game. Every fight is simulated moment by moment from both fighters’ skills, stamina, damage and momentum, and three judges score each round separately, so close fights can produce split decisions, just like real ones. The odds band you see before a fight comes from simulating that same fight many times with the same rules, and nothing hidden is tilted against you. Coin flips still land the other way half the time, and a puncher’s chance is a real chance. If a result looks impossible rather than unlucky, send the seed and it will be checked.

The game will not open, or opens to a black screen

Force-quit and reopen first. If it persists, reinstalling is safe to try but will delete your save — if you can still reach the end-of-career card, note the seed first. Then write in, with your phone model, and it will get looked at.

Content and age rating

Title Shot is intended for players aged 12 and over. It is about a combat sport, so it describes fights: strikes, knockdowns, submissions, injuries, weight cuts, and the long-term cost of taking damage. Fights are told as text, the way a commentator calls them — impact, not gore. There is no blood shown, no sexual content, no gambling and no strong language.

If you think a specific event crosses a line, please say which one. Events have ids and they can be changed or removed in an update. That feedback is genuinely wanted.

Are these real fighters or promotions?

No. Every fighter, coach, gym, promotion, title and commentator in the game is invented. The game is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any real promotion, league, sanctioning body or athlete. If anything in it reads as a real person or organisation to you, tell us and it will be rewritten.

Refunds

The game has no in-app purchases, so there is nothing to refund from us. If you paid for the app itself, refunds are handled by Apple through reportaproblem.apple.com, or by Google through the Play Store — we cannot issue those, but we would like to know what went wrong.

Privacy and data requests

There is no data to request. The app collects nothing and sends nothing; see the privacy policy. Your save is a file on your phone that you can read yourself.
