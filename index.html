TITLE SHOT

A whole fight career. No energy, no waiting. Fights you can read.

What it is

A life simulator about a mixed martial arts career. You are nineteen, fighting on the local amateur circuit for tickets you have to sell yourself. Fifteen years later you could be the undisputed champion, a gatekeeper the prospects have to get past, or the coach of your own gym. One career feed, one button, and fight nights you watch round by round.

You pick a background (kickboxer, college wrestler, BJJ black belt or street brawler) and climb from the amateurs to the regional Coastline Fighting Championship to the Global Fighting League and its fifteen-man rankings. Every rung is a fight you can lose.

A dentist offers $600 to put his name on your banner, if you wear his mouthguard. It has his phone number molded on the front.

  • Take the deal, wear the guard
  • Banner yes, mouthguard no
  • Keep your own guard

— one event, of more than two hundred

How it works

  • Fights you can read. Every fight is simulated moment by moment, and the moments that mattered come through as play-by-play. You set the game plan, make the corner call between rounds, and decide whether to swarm or stay patient when someone gets hurt.
  • Real judging. Three judges score every round on their own, so close fights go to split decisions and finishes come in rounds and minutes.
  • Camp. Pick a training focus and an intensity. Push too hard and you get hurt; train too light and he comes in sharper.
  • Choose your fights. Two or three offers each time, each with an honest odds band, a purse and a rank. Short notice pays more. Easy fights pay less.
  • A career with a shape. Damage you take early shows up late. After thirty the body starts to collect. At some point the question is “one more fight?”, and only you can answer it.
  • Twelve endings, from Undisputed to One fight too many. At the end, Corner Notes shows the real numbers: your career damage, your chin, and the odds you actually beat.

All fiction

Every fighter, gym, promotion, title and commentator in Title Shot is invented. No real athlete or organisation appears in the game, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by any promotion, league or sanctioning body.

What it does not do

No ads. No in-app purchases. No timers or energy. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no networking code at all — it works in aeroplane mode. Your career is a small file on your own phone. See the privacy policy, which is short because there is nothing to describe.
